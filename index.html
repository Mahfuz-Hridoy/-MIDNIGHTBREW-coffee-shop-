Our Legacy

The Midnight Brew Story

Founded in 2016, Midnight Brew was born out of a simple, intense obsession: roasting the highest quality single-origin beans and creating an elegant sanctuary for true coffee enthusiasts.

Every cup we brew is a meticulously crafted masterpiece. From importing shade-grown beans directly from micro-lot farms in Ethiopia and Colombia to roasting them locally in small batches, we control every detail of the process to unlock the ultimate sensory experience for you.
